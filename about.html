Our Story

From a home stall to Ha Tshifhiwa's favourite kota.

Tshifhiwa's Kota King started small — a few kotas made at home and sold to neighbours who kept coming back for more. Word spread fast, and what began as a side hustle grew into a proper local kota store, built one loyal customer at a time.

Mission

Serve it right, every time.

To serve high-quality, affordable kotas that are packed with flavour and made fresh, with fast and friendly service every single visit.

Vision

The name people trust.

To become the most loved and recognised kota store in the area — known for consistency, generous portions, and community spirit.

What Makes Us Different

No shortcuts, no small portions.

Fresh, every day

Bread is hollowed and fillings prepped fresh each day — nothing sits around.

Packed, not sprinkled

From polony to Russians, every layer is stacked generously, not scattered on top.

Local at heart

Built by and for the Ha Tshifhiwa / Madabani community — regulars are treated like family.
